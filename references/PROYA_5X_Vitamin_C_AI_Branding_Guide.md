# PROYA 5X Vitamin C Series — AI & E-Commerce Branding Guide

> **Purpose:** Master creative reference for all PROYA 5X Vitamin C Series assets created by humans or AI, including product photos, marketplace images, TikTok Shop / Shopee etalase, Instagram posts, TikTok story visuals, promotional graphics, website visuals, product knowledge documents, PDFs, educational posts, campaign key visuals, and other digital brand materials.
>
> **Rule:** When generating any visual or copy, preserve PROYA's product identity first. Creativity may change the scene, lighting, composition, props, and campaign concept — but **must not redesign the product packaging**.

---

### Brand
**PROYA**

**PROYA 5X Vitamin C Series**

PROYA should consistently feel:

- Bright
- Fresh
- Scientific
- Clean
- Gentle
- Premium
- Trustworthy
- Modern
- Benefit-led
- Approachable

# 2. CORE PRODUCT STORY

## 2.1 Main Technology

### 5X Vitamin C Derivatives
The core product story is the combination of five Vitamin C derivatives:

1. **3-O-Ethyl Ascorbic Acid**
2. **Ascorbyl Glucoside**
3. **Ascorbyl Palmitate**
4. **Ascorbyl Tetraisopalmitate**
5. **Magnesium Ascorbyl Phosphate**

### Core Communication Themes
For public-facing creative, prioritize these safer communication territories:

- Advanced Vitamin C derivative technology
- Brightening care
- Helps improve the look of dull skin
- Supports a more even-looking skin tone
- Hydration
- Gentle daily care
- Fresh glowing appearance
- Skin looks smoother and more radiant
- Supports healthy-looking skin

### Supporting Ingredients Across the Series
Depending on the product, supporting ingredients may include:

- Niacinamide
- Alpha-Arbutin / Arbutin
- Tranexamic Acid
- Ergothioneine
- Hyaluronic Acid
- Plant Extracts
- Carnosine
- Mandelic Acid — serum only, according to internal product knowledge

### Scientific Visual Language
When visualizing the technology, use:

- Golden molecular structures
- Transparent glass spheres
- Light refraction
- Vitamin C-inspired citrus glow
- Liquid bubbles
- Crystal-clear gel or water
- Fine laboratory glassware used decoratively, not medically
- Clean diagrams
- Thin line icons
- Soft halos of orange-gold light

Avoid:

- Medical syringes
- Hospital imagery
- Pills / capsules
- Blood / cells / anatomy visuals
- Harsh chemical lab environments
- Overly technical biotechnology imagery that makes the product appear medicinal

# 5. TYPOGRAPHY DIRECTION

> Exact font selection may vary by platform. The visual hierarchy is more important than forcing one specific typeface.

## 5.1 PROYA Wordmark

The official **PROYA logo must be used exactly as supplied whenever possible**.

Do not:

- Redraw the wordmark
- Change its letter shapes
- Replace it with a random font
- Stretch or compress it
- Add shadows, bevels, outlines, or metallic effects to the official logo

## 5.2 Headline Style

Use:

- Bold clean sans serif
- Strong geometric or modern grotesk feel
- Uppercase is preferred for short campaign headlines
- Large and immediately readable

Examples:

- `BRIGHTER LOOKING SKIN, EVERY DAY.`
- `5X VITAMIN C TECHNOLOGY`
- `HYDRATE. BRIGHTEN. GLOW.`
- `YOUR DAILY VITAMIN C ROUTINE`

## 5.3 Subheads

- Medium-weight sans serif
- Short
- Clean
- Supportive of the main message

## 5.4 Body Copy

- Simple sans serif
- Highly legible
- Short paragraphs
- Avoid tiny text
- Prefer 1–3 benefit bullets in promotional graphics

## 5.5 Text Hierarchy

Recommended order:

1. Main headline
2. Product / technology name
3. 1–3 key benefits
4. Supporting ingredient or usage note
5. CTA / offer / shop information

# 7. PACKAGING DNA — STRICT AI RULE

## 7.1 Packaging Identity

The products share a recognizable design system:

- Warm translucent / glossy orange-yellow bodies
- White caps, pumps, lids, closures, or droppers
- Black framed label area on most products
- Black PROYA logo
- Strong white `VITAMIN C` text
- Minimal clinical-inspired structure
- Smooth premium plastic / glass appearance
- Warm amber-orange liquid or body where applicable

## 7.2 Packaging Accuracy Priority

When creating AI images, packaging accuracy has **higher priority than scene creativity**.

### AI MUST preserve:

- Product category and silhouette
- Bottle / tube / jar proportions
- Cap style
- Pump / dropper style
- White closure color
- Orange body color
- Main label placement
- PROYA logo position
- `VITAMIN C` visual emphasis
- Overall front-label balance

### AI MUST NOT:

- Turn serum into a pump bottle
- Turn toner into a dropper
- Turn cleanser into a bottle
- Turn eye cream into a jar
- Change the sheet mask into a bottle
- Add luxury gold caps
- Add black caps
- Add new decorative patterns to packaging
- Make the packaging transparent glass when the reference does not look transparent
- Make the orange body look red, brown, or neon
- Invent new product names
- Randomly alter label typography

## 7.3 Product Reference Rule for AI

When a product reference image is available, always instruct the AI:

> **Use the attached product image as the exact packaging reference. Preserve the product's shape, proportions, cap, label layout, logo position, color, and packaging material. Do not redesign the packaging. Only change the environment, camera angle, lighting, props, and composition.**

# 11. COMPOSITION RULES

## Product-First Rule
The product should normally be the most important object in the image.

### Recommended Hierarchy

1. Product
2. Headline / offer
3. Product benefit
4. Supporting visual element
5. CTA / price / marketplace information

### Product Scale

For marketplace hero images:
- Product should occupy roughly **45–75%** of the main visual area depending on format.

For Instagram editorial images:
- Product may occupy **25–60%** depending on concept.

For TikTok / Story:
- Make the product large and immediately recognizable in the first glance.

### Negative Space
Leave deliberate empty space for:

- Headlines
- Price
- Discount badge
- Benefit bullets
- CTA

Do not fill every corner.
